## Upstream projects

This work extends CrocoDash and relies on CROCODILE-CESM, CESM, CDEPS, CMEPS,
MOM6, regional-mom6, and associated community software. Their developers
retain authorship of the upstream projects. The upstream CrocoDash citation is
available at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17342301.

## Data providers

The workflow uses ERA5 from ECMWF through NCAR RDA, GEBCO bathymetry, GLORYS
ocean fields, and TPXO tides. These datasets are not redistributed here.
